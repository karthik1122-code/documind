        How DocuMind answers

        1. ChunkPages are split at headings, so an answer stays in one piece.
        2. RetrieveKeyword search and a local semantic index each vote, then a reranker orders the top passages.
        3. Answer or refuseOnly from those passages, with the source marked. Unknown words or weak matches mean a refusal.

        Measured on a 50-question set written for this corpus. The tables and their limits are in the repository's eval/results.
