DocuMind Checking…

Retrieval-augmented Q&A · evaluated, not guessed

Ask the Express 5 docs.
Get the passage, not a guess.

Every answer points to the exact paragraph it came from. If the docs don't cover your question, DocuMind says so instead of making something up.

How it works

  1. ChunkPages are split at headings, so an answer stays in one piece.
  2. RetrieveKeyword search and a local semantic index vote, then a reranker orders the top passages.
  3. AnswerOnly from those passages, with citations. Unknown words or weak matches mean a refusal.

Measured on a 50-question set written for this corpus: see the repository's eval/results for the tables and their limits.
