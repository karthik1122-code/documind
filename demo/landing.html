    What this does not claim.

    Read these before you trust any of the numbers above.

    • Small benchmark. The questions were written by the author for one corpus. With 35 answerable questions, one question is 2.9 percentage points.
    • Paraphrases stay hard. Two questions are missed by every local method because they share almost no words with the answer. That is the case for a neural embedding model.
    • Thresholds are tuned on Express. On your own documents the refusals may be stricter or looser than ideal. I haven't measured it.
    • This preview is extractive. It copies the best sentence. It doesn't write answers, so it can't invent one.
