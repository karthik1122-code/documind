Every answer, marked at the source.

Ask the Express 5 documentation a question. DocuMind answers with the exact sentence, highlights it on the original page, and says "not in the docs" when it can't.

Runs in your browser. No account, no key, nothing leaves the page.

Errata: what this does not claim.

Read these before trusting any number above.

  1. 1Small benchmark. The questions were written by the author for one corpus. With 35 answerable questions, one question is 2.9 percentage points.
  2. 2Paraphrases stay hard. Two questions are missed by every local method because they share almost no words with the answer. That is the case for a neural embedding model.
  3. 3Thresholds are tuned on Express. On your own documents the refusals may be stricter or looser than ideal. I haven't measured it.
  4. 4This preview is extractive. It copies the best sentence. It doesn't write answers, so it can't invent one.
